Why these are smooth and the old one is not. The old design repainted the full-screen gradient and a wide soft glow on every single frame — about four million blended pixels, sixty times a second, which overran the phone's budget and dropped roughly one frame in twenty. Here the background and glow never change, so the phone draws them once and re-stamps the result; the only thing redrawn each frame is a thin outline or two.
