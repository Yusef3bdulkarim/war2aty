What changed from variant C

  • Top bar: C's floating ✕ is replaced with TealTopBar as-is: the back arrow at the start, no title, bottom corners rounded 28, the status bar inside the teal. It stays on the opening and error states, the same way it does on the analysis failure pages.
  • Smaller controls: the shutter is 68 (from 78) with a 3px teal ring. Photos and flash are 44 discs (from 52) inside 48 × 48 tap areas.
  • Controls never cover the paper: the capsule, the hint and the flash status all sit in the dock below the feed.
  • Flash: each tap moves off → auto → on. The icon changes shape for each mode (slashed bolt, bolt with «A», plain bolt) and a status chip spells it out for 2 s. When the shot is taken with the flash on, or on auto in low light, the flash fires. Coming back to the camera resets it to off.
  • Hint: «المس الورقة علشان تبقى واضحة» fades in when the camera is ready. It goes away after 4 s or at the first tap on the paper.
  • No flash on the phone: the button is gone completely, and the capsule shifts so the shutter stays centred.
  • Camera error: «اختار صورة من الموبايل» is added under «حاول تاني».

Still to pick before T10

  1. Layout: C1 (framed window) or C2 (edge-to-edge).
  2. Top bar colour: brand teal #0E7C86, identical to the analysis and error screens, or deep teal #0A5C64. Deep teal is calmer next to a live camera and still from the palette, but it would differ from the other bars.
  3. Bottom area:
    • Glass capsule: as in C.
    • Mirrored dock: a panel whose rounded top echoes the bar's rounded bottom, framing the feed between the two.
    • Teal glow: a soft brand glow behind a teal-tinted capsule.
  4. When the hint shows: the mockup shows it every time the camera opens, including coming back from the crop screen. The alternative is once per camera visit: not again after a retake.
