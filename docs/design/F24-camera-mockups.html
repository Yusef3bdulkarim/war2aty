The phone is live: tap the paper to focus, tap ⚡ to change the flash, and tap the shutter. The earlier pages (A / B / C, and the refinement options) are in git history.
State

Layout (top to bottom)

Top barTealTopBar as-is: brand teal #0E7C86, bottom corners 28, the status bar inside it, the back arrow («رجوع») at the start, no title. It stays on the opening and error states.
FeedFull width, 8px under the bar, Fit (3:4 here). Nothing is drawn over it except the focus brackets. On a short screen the feed shrinks, still Fit, so the dock keeps its height.
DockThe space below the feed: the status line (the hint or the flash mode), then the capsule.
CapsulePhotos · shutter · flash in RTL order. Translucent white 6% fill with a 13% hairline border. Discs are 44 inside 48 × 48 tap areas. The shutter is 68 with a 3px teal ring and a faint outer ring.

Behaviour

FlashEach tap moves off → auto → on, and the icon changes shape (slashed bolt / bolt with «A» / bolt; on also fills mint). The status line spells out «الفلاش: …» for 2 s. The flash fires with the shutter, and it is off every time the camera opens, including after a retake.
No flash on the phoneThe flash button is not shown. The capsule shifts so the shutter stays centred.
Tap to focusFocus (and exposure, where the phone supports it) on the tapped point. White corner brackets with a dark halo show where, then fade after ~1.4 s.
Hint«المس الورقة علشان تبقى واضحة» shows once per camera visit, when the camera first opens. It hides after 4 s or at the first tap. It does not come back after a retake or a retry.
PhotosOpens the phone's photo picker in place of the camera, so Back returns Home (same as the permission sheet's «اختار صورة بدلًا من ذلك»).
Camera errorThe bar, the message, «حاول تاني», then «اختار صورة من الموبايل».
